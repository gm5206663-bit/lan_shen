### 🔴 THE LOAD-BEARING RULE

> *"The higher the grade of spirit souls, the higher a spirit master's spiritual power must be
> in order to absorb it."*
> *"A spirit master with Spirit Origin intermediate ranked spiritual power will be able to fuse
> with a yellow spirit soul."*

Spiritual power gates which soul spirit you may contract. **This is the one door rank-one soul
power does not close**, and it is why the premise works mechanically.

### Soul spirits — the SL3 ring system

In SL3 soul beasts are nearly extinct, so soul masters obtain **soul spirits** rather than
hunting rings. Four canonical acquisition routes:

```
1. geniuses with exceedingly fast cultivation  → first soul spirit FREE from the Spirit Pagoda
2. ordinary individuals                        → PURCHASE, fee proportional to the spirit's age
3. contract with a soul beast                  → BY MUTUAL AGREEMENT
4. the Tower Attack (Spirit Ascension Platform) → won as a reward
```

Soul spirits are **superior to conventional rings** because their age/quality can be upgraded
or evolved — at the Spirit Pagoda's **Spirit Ascension Platform**, generally only up to peak
**Black** rank. There are **eighteen** platforms on the Douluo Continent. If a soul spirit
voluntarily leaves, the contracted master **permanently loses those rings**.

### Spirit Pagoda (Chuan Ling Ta) — the far spine, canonically

- Founded by **Huo Yuhao** (SL2's protagonist) with Di Tian, from Electrolux's contract
  technique — *"a life-bound contract method derived from light element necromancy."*
- Its canonical remit: **"awakening Martial Souls across the continent, conducting spiritual
  power tests and cultivation, and registering Battle Armor Masters."** 🔴 It runs the exact
  ceremony chapter one is set in.
- Council Hall in **Shrek City**. Pagoda Masters have included **Gu Yuena**.
- 🔴 Its history is not clean: *"originally established to mediate peace between humans and
  soul beasts. However, for the sake of their research into the invention of artificial spirit
  souls, there was a period of time where the Spirit Pagoda slaughtered countless soul beasts
  with the Federation's support."*
- Artificial spirits are built from **soul beast genes**, and *"as Soul Beasts gradually became
  extinct, their genes were also continuously being lost."*
